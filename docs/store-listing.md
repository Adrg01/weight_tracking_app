## Full Description

**Stop being fooled by your scale.**

Your weight fluctuates 1-3 kg every single day. Water retention, meals, time of day — all of it adds noise that hides your real progress. Scaley uses Bayesian statistical modeling to cut through the noise and show you what your weight actually is.

**How Scaley is different:**

Weigh yourself any time — morning, evening, after lunch — it doesn't matter. Scaley learns your body's daily pattern and automatically adjusts for time-of-day fluctuations. You get one clean number: your estimated true weight, with a confidence interval.

**Key features:**

- Bayesian weight estimation — AI-powered filtering removes daily fluctuations
- BMI tracking with visual scale indicator
- Interactive weight chart — tap any point for detailed breakdown
- Goal tracking — see exactly how far you are from your target
- 60+ science-backed tips tailored to your goal (lose, gain, or maintain)
- Smart reminders — notifies you during your idle time, skips days you've already logged
- CSV data export — your data, your control
- Light & dark themes
- kg/lbs and cm/ft+in support
- 100% offline — all data stays on your device

**For weight loss:**
Track your journey with precision. See past the daily ups and downs. Know exactly when you're making progress — not just hoping.

**For weight gain:**
Building mass? Scaley tracks your upward trend and adjusts for daily water weight dips that might discourage you.

**For maintenance:**
Already at your goal? Monitor stability with confidence intervals that tell you if you're truly holding steady.

**Privacy first:**
All your data is stored locally on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to any server. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Download Scaley and see your real weight for the first time.
