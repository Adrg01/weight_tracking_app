Scaley Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 11, 2026

Scaley ("we", "our", "the app") is a weight tracking application. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

The app collects the following information that you provide directly:

  • Weight measurements and timestamps
  • Height, age, and gender
  • Goal weight
  • Wake and sleep times
  • Unit and theme preferences

How Your Data Is Stored

All personal data is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. We do not operate any servers that receive, store, or process your personal data. Your weight, health, and profile information never leaves your phone.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data for ad personalization, including:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General usage data

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android). For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into the app is Google AdMob for advertising. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or any other third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your measurement data at any time through Settings > Clear History within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

Data Export

You can export all your measurement data as a CSV file through Settings > Export Data. This file is generated locally and shared via your device's share functionality.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Access: All your data is visible directly in the app.
  • Deletion: Use Clear History in Settings to delete your data.
  • Portability: Use Export Data in Settings to download your data as CSV.

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we have no access to it, these rights are exercised directly through the app.

Your Rights (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. All data is stored locally on your device. We have no access to your personal data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact
